What is Neeva

A calm pause inside the apps where you shop.

Neeva is a pop-up companion that lives inside shopping apps. At checkout it asks two quick questions and shows a Future Receipt, so you can weigh what you need against what it's worth before you pay.

It is not an anti-shopping app. It never shames anyone. It gives people a short, calm pause, so the next purchase is one they meant to make.

The core feature · Future Receipt

Before you pay, see what it will really cost you.

A normal receipt tells you what you spent after buying. Neeva's Future Receipt comes before. It compares what you expect with how you've actually used similar things, and turns the price into a cost per wear.

Brand · colour

Two colours from the logo do most of the talking.

Sale screens shout in red and yellow to rush you. Neeva does the opposite: soft, low-contrast tints that slow the eye down and lower the pressure.
